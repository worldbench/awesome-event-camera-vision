\begin{abstract}
Event cameras are bio-inspired neuromorphic sensors that asynchronously capture per-pixel brightness changes with microsecond temporal resolution, high dynamic range, and low power consumption. This survey reviews event camera vision in the era of large models, spanning perception, reconstruction, understanding, applications, datasets, and other resources. We organize recent advances with particular attention to vision transformers, diffusion models, state-space models, neural radiance fields, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and multimodal foundation models. We further summarize representative datasets and benchmarks, analyze the evolution from task-specific architectures toward pre-trained and generalizable models, and identify open challenges for robust, efficient, and broadly deployable event-based vision. A continuously updated companion repository is provided to support the community.
\end{abstract}
